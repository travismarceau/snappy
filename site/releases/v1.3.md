# Snappy 1.3

## New

- **See where your window will land.** As you drag a region in the placement grid, a faint highlight previews its destination on that display. It updates immediately as your selection changes, includes your margins and gaps, and stays behind the selector without taking focus or intercepting clicks.

## Fixed

- Pressing Escape fully cancels a drag and clears the preview. Continuing to move the mouse with the button held no longer restarts the canceled selection.
- Each placement grid uses its own monitor's bounds, including when combined display mode is enabled. Windows with size constraints also stay aligned to the chosen display.

Requires macOS 12 or later.
